# Demo walkthrough

Start the synthetic sample using the README. Its geometric images verify the workflow; they do not illustrate real styling.

1. Open My wardrobe. Explain that items have categories and stable codes.
2. Open the blue sample top's details, then follow its linked outfit to show the relationship between garments and outfits.
3. Return to My wardrobe and choose Not in any outfits. The sample jacket is not in the sample outfit and will appear here.
4. Show the external chat or maintenance command separately. The website has no photo upload or AI generation button; do not imply otherwise.
5. To demonstrate Ready to let go, first query the sample jacket's current code. Use the status command in the maintenance guide against the sample server's origin and data directory only.

When recording real clothes, review prepared images against the originals. AI images help compare combinations; they cannot verify size, fabric or actual fit.
